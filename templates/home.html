ABOUT PROJECTVOTE

A project exploring digital democracy

ProjectVote is an academic project designed to explore the concepts involved in building an online voting system.

The project focuses on the complete voting workflow, from voter registration and identity verification to election management, candidate selection, vote confirmation and result publication.

Django is used as the web framework, while email OTP verification and face verification demonstrate additional layers that can be incorporated into a digital voting workflow.

The goal of ProjectVote is not to replace official government election infrastructure. Instead, it serves as a practical exploration of the technologies, workflows and security considerations involved in online voting.
