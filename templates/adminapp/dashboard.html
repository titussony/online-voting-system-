Voter Management

Registered voter information is maintained within the ProjectVote system. Election eligibility is managed separately for each election, allowing administrators to select the appropriate voters without removing existing user accounts.

Election Archive

Closed elections are removed from the active Manage Elections section and preserved in Election History. Their election details, candidates, recorded votes, eligibility records and published results remain available for historical reference.

Administration Overview

ProjectVote provides a centralized administration workflow for preparing elections, managing candidates, configuring voter eligibility, controlling election status and reviewing results. Use the management sections above to perform administrative operations.
